Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026 • Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how SupoClip ("SupoClip", "we", "our", or "us") collects, utilizes, stores, and safeguards your personal data when you interact with our website, application, and video generation & publishing tools (the "Service").

SupoClip is an AI-powered video processing platform that turns long-form content into vertical, captioned clips and enables users to publish them directly to connected third-party social media accounts, including TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a securely salted password hash. Plaintext passwords are never recorded or stored.

User-Provided Content

To produce clips, we process the video URLs you provide (e.g., YouTube or direct video links) and video files you upload. We store the derived clips, transcriptions, subtitles, and processing metadata so you can preview, edit, schedule, or download them.

Connected Social Accounts (OAuth Data)

When you connect an external social account to SupoClip, authentication is conducted securely via OAuth 2.0 directly on the provider's official authorization portal. We collect and store:

  • Basic profile identification (e.g., account display name, handle, avatar URL).
  • Encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Post identifiers and aggregate performance metrics (such as views, likes, and comment counts) for clips published via SupoClip, used exclusively to optimize clip selection.

We never receive, see, or store your passwords for any third-party social platform.

3. Third-Party Platform Integrations

Our Service integrates with developer APIs from TikTok, Meta (Instagram), and Google (YouTube):

TikTok

We use TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Scopes requested include user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.list. We only use these permissions to authenticate your identity, publish video clips you explicitly approve or schedule, and check upload status. We do not sell or distribute TikTok user data.

Instagram (Meta)

We use the official Meta Instagram Graph API / Instagram Content Publishing API for professional (Business / Creator) accounts. We access the user ID, username, and publishing endpoints to upload selected Reels. In compliance with Meta developer requirements, videos are delivered via temporary, secured media URLs directly to Meta's servers.

YouTube (Google)

When connecting YouTube, SupoClip uses YouTube Data API v3 services. By using this integration, you acknowledge and agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or revoke SupoClip's access to your Google account at any time via Google Security Settings.

4. How We Use and Share Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:

  • Operating the video clipping, subtitling, and AI analysis pipeline.
  • Publishing approved clips to your connected social channels upon your direct command or schedule.
  • Displaying post analytics and performance indicators within your personal dashboard.
  • Maintaining platform security, error logging, and debugging.

No Data Sale: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data, media, or social platform tokens to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

5. Security & Token Storage

All third-party OAuth tokens (for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube) are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption keys before being committed to our database. All client-server communications occur over HTTPS (TLS 1.3/1.2).

6. Data Retention and Deletion Instructions

We retain your personal data and uploaded files only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. You maintain total control over your data:

Disconnecting Social Accounts

  • From SupoClip: Navigate to Settings → Social Accounts and click Disconnect next to the respective platform. This immediately deletes the stored OAuth credentials from our database.
  • From TikTok: In the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, locate SupoClip, and click Remove access.
  • From Instagram / Facebook: Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, locate SupoClip, and click Remove.
  • From YouTube / Google: Visit Google Account Permissions and remove SupoClip access.

Requesting Full Account & Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your SupoClip account, all associated video clips, logs, and personal data, send an email to:

We will verify your request and completely purge all personal records, media files, and linked tokens within 30 days of confirmation, in compliance with GDPR, LGPD, and CCPA standards.
